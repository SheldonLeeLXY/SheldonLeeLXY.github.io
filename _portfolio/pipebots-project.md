The **Pipebots Project** developed miniature autonomous robots for inspection, localization, and mapping in buried pipe networks. During my MPhil at the University of Leeds, I worked on robot navigation, topological mapping, embedded systems, and multi-sensor integration for confined underground environments.

My work combined Linux/ROS development, Gazebo simulation, camera and IMU sensing, and real-pipe physical experiments to support robust robotic operation in limited-visibility pipe networks.

Links: [Project Website](https://pipebots.ac.uk/), [Publication](https://doi.org/10.3389/frobt.2023.1202568).
