The **PAVE Dataset** is a real-world autonomous-driving dataset built for system-level evaluation of production autonomous vehicles. It is collected in naturalistic driving environments and integrates synchronized camera, GNSS/RTK, IMU, and CAN bus data.

The dataset supports evaluation beyond perception-only benchmarks by distinguishing autonomous driving and manual driving modes, enabling analysis of real-world autonomous vehicle behavior, safety, and trajectory quality.

Links: [Project Page](https://kite-lab.github.io/PAVE_Dataset/), [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14185).
