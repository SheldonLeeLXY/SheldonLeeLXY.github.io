PAVE is a real-world autonomous-driving dataset designed for system-level evaluation of production autonomous vehicles. The dataset integrates camera, GNSS/RTK, IMU, and CAN bus data collected in naturalistic driving environments, and distinguishes autonomous driving and manual driving modes for evaluation of real-world autonomous vehicle behavior.
