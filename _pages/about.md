I am **Xiangyu Li**, a PhD student at **The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)**. My research focuses on autonomous driving systems, perception and localization, end-to-end driving models, and system-level evaluation using large-scale real-world driving datasets.

Before joining HKUST(GZ), I completed an MPhil in Robotics at the **University of Leeds**, where I worked on real-time localization and topological mapping for buried pipe networks. I also received a BSc in Computer Science and Technology from **Southwest Jiaotong University (SWJTU)**.

Research Interests
======
- Autonomous driving systems and production autonomous vehicle evaluation
- Perception, localization, and trajectory quality analysis
- End-to-end driving models and real-world driving datasets
- Robotics in confined spaces, autonomous navigation, and topological mapping

Selected Work
======
- **[PAVE Dataset](https://kite-lab.github.io/PAVE_Dataset/)**: building a large-scale real-world autonomous driving dataset collected in naturalistic driving environments with camera, GNSS/RTK, IMU, and CAN bus data.
- **[Pipebots Project](https://pipebots.ac.uk/)**: research on miniature autonomous robots for inspection and mapping in underground pipe networks, including navigation, localization, and multi-sensor integration on Linux/ROS platforms.
